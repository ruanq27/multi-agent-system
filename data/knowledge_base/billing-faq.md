# Billing FAQ

## Subscription Plans

### Plans Overview
- **Starter**: $9.99/month - Perfect for individuals
- **Professional**: $24.99/month - For small teams
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing - For large organizations

### What's Included
All plans include:
- 24/7 customer support
- Automatic backups
- Email support
- API access (rate limited)
- Basic analytics

Professional and Enterprise tiers include:
- Priority support
- Advanced analytics
- Unlimited API calls
- Custom integrations
- Dedicated account manager (Enterprise only)

## Payment Information

### Payment Methods Accepted
- Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- PayPal
- Wire transfer (Enterprise only)

### Billing Cycle
- Monthly subscriptions renew on the same date each month
- Annual subscriptions provide 20% discount and renew yearly

## Refund Policy

### 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee
If you're not satisfied with our service within the first 30 days, we'll provide a full refund.

### How to Request a Refund
1. Contact our support team with your account email
2. Provide the reason for the refund request
3. We'll process the refund within 5-7 business days

### Refund Restrictions
- Refunds only available within 30 days of initial purchase
- Cannot refund if service was used extensively
- No refunds on service credits

## Upgrades and Downgrades

### Upgrading Your Plan
- Upgrades are processed immediately
- You'll be charged the pro-rated difference
- All changes effective immediately

### Downgrading Your Plan
- Downgrades take effect at next billing cycle
- You can downgrade anytime via account settings
- No penalties or fees for downgrading

## Invoices

### Viewing Invoices
- Access all invoices in your Account → Billing section
- Download invoices as PDF for records

### Invoice Details
Each invoice includes:
- Invoice number and date
- Description of services
- Amount charged
- Payment method used
- Due date (usually immediately)

## Common Issues

### Credit Card Declined
If your payment fails:
1. Verify card details are correct
2. Check card has sufficient funds
3. Try again after 24 hours
4. Use alternative payment method

### Missing Invoice
- Invoices are generated automatically
- Check spam folder for email notifications
- Contact support if invoice is not found

## Tax Information
- Prices shown exclude applicable taxes
- Tax calculated at checkout based on location
- Tax included on invoice for reference
