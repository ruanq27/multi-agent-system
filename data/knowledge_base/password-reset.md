# How to Reset Password

## Quick Solution
If you can't access your account, follow these steps to reset your password:

1. Click the **"Forgot Password"** link on the login page
2. Enter the email address associated with your account
3. Check your email (including spam folder) for a password reset link
4. Click the link in the email to proceed with the reset
5. Create a new strong password (minimum 8 characters, include uppercase, numbers, and symbols)
6. Confirm your new password
7. Log in with your new credentials

## If You Don't Receive the Email

- **Check spam/junk folder**: Password reset emails sometimes get filtered
- **Wait 5 minutes**: Email delivery can take a few minutes
- **Try again**: Request another reset link if the first one expires
- **Contact support**: If you still don't receive the email, contact our support team

## Security Tips
- Never share your password with anyone
- Use a unique password not used on other sites
- Enable two-factor authentication for added security
- Change your password regularly (every 90 days recommended)

## Still Having Issues?
If you continue to experience problems, please contact our support team with:
- Your email address
- The error message you're seeing (if any)
- Your browser and device information
